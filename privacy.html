Arrow Relay Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Last Pixel Games, the publisher of Arrow Relay, handles information in the Android game and on this policy page. You can play the puzzles offline without creating an account.

Game data on your device

Arrow Relay stores your board progress, completed levels, credits and rewards, and settings in the app’s private storage on your device. The game uses these records to resume play, restore your preferences, and award credits once for each qualifying reward. Arrow Relay does not upload your saved boards or credit balance to the publisher’s servers.

Optional rewarded ads

When you choose Watch ad, Arrow Relay uses Google AdMob to request a rewarded ad. The game uses the ad completion signal to add credits to your device’s balance.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address, approximate location inferred from that address, device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID where available, interactions with the app or ads, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for purposes such as delivering and measuring ads, analytics, and fraud prevention. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where applicable, Google’s consent service presents ad privacy choices. If Settings → Ad privacy is available in the game, you can use it to review or change those choices. You can keep playing and restart puzzles without watching ads.

Retention and deletion

The game keeps local records so you can pick up where you left off. Settings → Reset progress clears the active attempt and completed levels while keeping earned credits. To erase the local app data, including credits and settings, clear Arrow Relay’s storage in Android Settings. Google handles information collected by its services according to its privacy policy.

This website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

For questions or requests about privacy, email gameslastpixel@gmail.com. If you contact us, we use your email address and any information you choose to provide to respond and follow up. We keep that correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations.
